- 🤝 **Community Learning** — Ask questions, share solutions, review approaches, and help other developers.

## 📏 Community Guidelines

To keep CodeFix Academy useful and welcoming for everyone:

1. **Be respectful.** Treat every member with kindness, regardless of experience level.
2. **Ask clear questions.** Explain the expected behavior, actual behavior, error messages, and relevant code.
3. **Share reproducible examples.** Provide minimal code that demonstrates the issue whenever possible.
4. **Explain your solutions.** Help others understand why a solution works, not just what to copy.
5. **Keep discussions relevant.** Post in the most appropriate category and use descriptive titles.
6. **Respect intellectual property.** Share code and content you have permission to publish.
7. **Protect privacy and security.** Never post passwords, API keys, access tokens, personal information, or confidential company code.
8. **Avoid spam.** Promotional content should be relevant and follow the community's rules.
9. **Give constructive feedback.** Critique ideas and code, not people.
10. **Learn together.** Beginners are welcome, and experienced developers are encouraged to share their knowledge.
